- C4 (supersedes the factual wording in PLAN section 4 step 4). It is NOT established that FFTW c2r generally discards imaginary Nyquist terms. R2C Hermitian constraints on stored half-spectra apply to the self-conjugate columns (kx=0 and kx=Nyquist) and the four corner bins; the y-Nyquist row at interior kx has no additional stored-pair constraint. NumPy `irfft2` completion (c2c over axis 0, then c2r over axis 1) is a declared auxiliary operator, not an asserted equivalent of native FFTW for invalid input. Its agreement with native is only what is MEASURED: captured native real maps vs `irfft2(captured consumed S)` (all five frames, both patches), and bridge-vs-captured stage errors. No generic invalid-C2R extension is assumed.
- C5 (supersedes PLAN section 6 `B_twin`). `B_ctrl` is a rigorous spectral comparison for the noisy control only (bridge-vs-captured, same input). It does not bound a different input, and it cannot cover the unretained per-iteration global deltas (single total-G ramp vs native multi-step float32 accumulation). Equal pedestal does not prove equal rounding error. Therefore no twin interval or sign certification is claimed. `B32` vs `B64` twin differences are reported as descriptive precision sensitivity only. Phase B is a conditional experiment under the two declared single-total-G NumPy bridges (B32, B64). Every twin conclusion is worded "under this auxiliary bridge"; no native-noise causation and no native-twin sign certification. The exact native limit is stated in the report.
