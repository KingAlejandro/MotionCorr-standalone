## Fit and supported scope

`--experimental_local_b2` defaults off. CPU `--use_own`, group_frames exactly 1, bin_factor exactly 1, no interpolate_shifts. Refuse GPU before querying a device and refuse other grouping/binning/interpolation. The unit of time is a decoded movie frame; EER detector subframes are not separately fitted. Global alignment, patch extraction, convergence, observation recentering, stored patch coordinates/times, reconstruction and STAR writer are unchanged.

Fit physical h(f) = sum c_sk S_s f^k (S=1,u,u²,v,v²,uv; k=1..3). Existing stored observation time is g+.5, so fit at g=stored_time-.5. Eliminate a free constant per patch/axis by orthogonal patch demeaning; column-scaled pivoted Householder QR solves the projected 18-column design in double precision (no normal equations). Require spatial rank6, >=4 distinct integer times per patch, rank18 after projection, equivalent to full rank18+npatch. Reject nonfinite/rank-deficient fits explicitly. No observations remains the existing no-local case. Physical h(0) is exactly zero; nuisance constants are neither applied nor published. B1's extra .5*S is absorbed by the nuisance and has the same physical solution. Midpoint q fitted at g+.5 must instead publish q(f+.5)-q(.5); publishing q(f) is a deliberate failing mutant.

The existing fit RMS rejection threshold 10px is unchanged. Under B2 only, RMS is the nuisance-adjusted observation residual; published localFit values are the physical h(g), without nuisance. Thus the diagnostic residual intentionally changes meaning under the flag. Existing spatial-neighbour validity checks and failure/publication behavior outside the flag remain unchanged.
