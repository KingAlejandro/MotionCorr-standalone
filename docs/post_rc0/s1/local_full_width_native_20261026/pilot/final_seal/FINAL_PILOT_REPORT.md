## Evidence separation and limits
Native evidence: the 16 real-binary runs above. Synthetic evidence: the driver/consumer tests (fake child, crafted scores), which establish consumer and driver logic only (the "synthetic" wording in the DECISION scope refers to those tests). The noisy case is **one exposed realization**: no noise-origin causation, no p-values, no independent-seed claim.
Historical N5/9/13/20 and harm evidence is retained unchanged; no new qualification of those rows. **Completed:** fixed-ROI field scoring (72x104 px x 5 frames, runtime and actual STAR) of all 16 runs; noisy still FAILs the .20/.40/.80 contract in both arms, flat and repeated are raw FAIL / UNIDENTIFIABLE. **Not scored:** pixels outside the fixed ROI. **Not tested:** applied-image accuracy or consistency, image information content, real data. Product compatibility is bounded under the predefined comparison described above, not byte neutrality. Also not established: GPU behaviour, any default or adoption change. full100, GPU and adoption remain UNRUN/held. No evidence comment, watcher or merge was made.
